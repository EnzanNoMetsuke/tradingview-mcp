# SMB Capital Trading Playbook

## Overview

Based on SMB Capital’s desk activity and training over the last 90 days, their
methodology has evolved into a "Prop-Tech" hybrid, combining institutional
earnings buckets with AI-driven infrastructure.

The following strategies and rules have been distilled from their core desk
strategies (The Earnings Playbook) and their updated risk management frameworks
for 2026.

### **The "Four Buckets" Earnings Strategy**

The core of SMB’s current swing and day-trading edge revolves around
categorizing earnings reports into specific "buckets" to determine the
probability of a multi-day rerate.

- **Bucket 1: The First Clean Profitable Beat:** This is the first quarter a
  company proves its business model by turning a profit (EPS positive). It
  signals to the Street that the company is sustainable.
- **Bucket 2: The Acceleration/Inflection Quarter:** This is the "Bread and
  Butter" A+ setup. Look for a massive jump in the *rate of change* of revenue
  and EPS growth compared to previous quarters. It provides the highest future
  revenue visibility.
- **Bucket 3: The "Slayer" (Continuing to Crush It):** Strong companies that
  continue to beat. While these are good, the "surprise factor" is often lower
  because the move was priced in during the Bucket 2 inflection.
- **Bucket 4: The Hot Theme Getting Rewarded (The 2026 Amendment):** This is a
  meta-strategy where individual stock metrics matter less than sector momentum.
  If the first 2-3 companies in a theme (e.g., Fiber Optics or Edge Computing)
  report strong numbers and follow through, the Street is rerating the entire
  basket. You can "break the rules" on individual technicals if the theme
  conviction is high.

### **The Quantitative "In Play" Scanner**

To filter hundreds of reports down to 1–3 daily candidates, the desk uses a
specific quantitative trigger called **% AVAL** (Percent of Average Volume).

- **The 20% Rule:** Any stock doing less than 20% of its average daily volume in
  the pre-market/after-hours session is generally ignored for earnings plays.
- **The 50%+ Trigger:** Stocks doing 50% to 200%+ of their average daily volume
  in the pre-market are considered "High Interest." This institutional footprint
  is a leading indicator of an intraday trend.
- **Execution Signal:** The primary "Go" signal is a high-volume break of the
  **Pre-market High** within the first 30 minutes of the open.

### **The AI Operational Edge**

A recurring theme in the last 90 days is the transition of elite traders into
"Tier 3" users who use LLMs (specifically Claude) as infrastructure partners
rather than for prediction.

- **Custom Alert Coding:** Use AI to write Pine Script (TradingView) or
  ThinkScript (Thinkorswim) for surgical alerts. A key desk alert: *Price breaks
  30-min Opening Range High + Volume > 1.5x average + Price > VWAP*.
- **Automated Autopsies:** Instead of a generic journal, feed trade screenshots
  and raw data into an LLM to identify "Performance by Hour." Many traders found
  their edge disappears completely after 11:30 AM, identifying a need to size
  down or stop trading during the "lunchtime" hours.
- **Game Plan Automation:** Save 45 minutes of morning prep by using AI to
  prioritize a watchlist based on overnight news, catalyst strength, and
  priority ranking (High/Medium/Low).

### **Risk Management: The "Third Rail" Rules**

The desk emphasizes that account blowups are preceded by seven "red flags." The
most critical rules for preventing a death spiral include:

- **The Max Bullets Rule:** Determine how many trades you take on your best days
  (typically 3–5). Set this as your hard limit. If you hit your "bullets," you
  must walk away from the station to avoid the overtrading trap.
- **The Negotiable Rule (Third Rail):** If you break your max loss rule once, it
  is "Permission Granted" for your brain to ignore rules again. You must freeze
  the account for that day *and* the following day to reset your psychology.
- **The Spectator Rule:** If you find yourself watching your P&L more than the
  price action (tabbing over to the P&L page), you are "spectating your own
  fear." Hide the P&L window or size down until you can execute 10 trades based
  purely on the chart.
- **Strategy Hopping:** Recognize that strategies don't "stop working"—market
  conditions simply change. A momentum strategy failing in a choppy market is a
  condition problem, not a strategy failure. Do not abandon the setup; wait for
  the environment to shift back.
